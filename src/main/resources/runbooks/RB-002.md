# RB-002: Price mismatch

## Applies when
Our price and the counterparty price differ, and quantity and settlement date agree.

## Tolerance
If the price difference is 0.1% or less of our price, treat it as rounding and propose ACCEPT_WITHIN_TOLERANCE. The difference is calculated by code and given to you. Do not calculate it yourself.

## Steps when outside tolerance
1. Look for a digit transposition or a decimal slip (for example 102.40 against 120.40).
2. Check the execution report for the agreed price.
3. If our booking is wrong, propose an amendment of our booking (needs an approver).
4. If the counterparty is wrong, contact their operations desk with the execution report.

## Proposed action
ACCEPT_WITHIN_TOLERANCE, CONTACT_COUNTERPARTY, or AMEND_OUR_BOOKING.
