# RB-006: Settlement date mismatch

## Applies when
Quantity and price agree, but our settlement date and the counterparty settlement date differ.

## Steps
1. Check the market settlement convention for the instrument. ASX equities settle on T+2.
2. Check for a public holiday that moves the date.
3. If our date breaks the convention, propose an amendment of our booking (needs an approver).
4. If the counterparty date breaks the convention, contact their operations desk.

## Proposed action
CONTACT_COUNTERPARTY, or AMEND_OUR_BOOKING.
