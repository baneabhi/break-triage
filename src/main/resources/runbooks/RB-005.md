# RB-005: Duplicate trade

## Applies when
Our system holds two bookings with the same counterparty, instrument, side, quantity, price and date, entered close together under different trade IDs, and the counterparty reports one trade.

## Steps
1. Confirm with the trader that only one trade was done.
2. Keep the earlier booking and propose cancelling the later one.
3. Cancel only after the trader confirms. This needs a named approver.

## Proposed action
CANCEL_DUPLICATE_PENDING_TRADER. Never cancel without trader confirmation.
