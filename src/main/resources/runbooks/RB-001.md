# RB-001: Quantity mismatch

## Applies when
Our quantity and the counterparty quantity differ, and price and settlement date agree.

## Not this runbook
- If the note says we hold two bookings of the same trade, use RB-005 (duplicate trade), even though the quantities differ.

## Steps
1. Compare our quantity with the counterparty's. If one is exactly 10x or 100x the other, a keying error is likely.
2. Check the original execution report or trader ticket for the true quantity.
3. If our booking is wrong, propose an amendment of our booking (needs an approver).
4. If the counterparty is wrong, contact their operations desk with the execution report.

## Proposed action
CONTACT_COUNTERPARTY, or AMEND_OUR_BOOKING if the execution report shows our quantity is wrong.
