# RB-003: Missing or invalid standing settlement instructions (SSI)

## Applies when
The trade matches on quantity, price and date, but settlement cannot proceed because the counterparty's SSI is missing, expired, rejected by the custodian, or points to an old account.

## Steps
1. Check static data for an SSI record for this counterparty and instrument.
2. If none exists or it has expired, request a new SSI from the counterparty through the static data team.
3. If the custodian rejected the format, ask the static data team to repair the instruction.
4. The deadline is the business day before settlement. After that the trade is at risk of failing.

## Proposed action
REQUEST_SSI. Do not amend the trade itself.
