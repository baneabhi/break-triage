# RB-004: Unconfirmed trade

## Applies when
The counterparty has not confirmed or affirmed the trade, so there are no counterparty quantity or price values to compare.

## Steps
1. Check whether our confirmation was sent and delivered.
2. Chase the counterparty's operations desk. First chase on T+1.
3. If still unconfirmed on T+2, tell the operations manager.
4. Do not amend or cancel the trade because of silence alone.

## Proposed action
CHASE_CONFIRMATION.
